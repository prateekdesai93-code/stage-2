Batch sizes (from your BOM) ›

One product per line: Name = Size Unit — e.g. Decor Cream = 1500 L or Oxide Black = 3000 KG. This is the standard batch quantity from your Bills of Materials. The ledger totals demand across every pack size (20L, 5L, 1L, 500ML…) into one litre/kg figure per product, then divides by this to tell you exactly how many batches to run — as a decimal, so 1.18 means one full batch plus a scaled-down partial batch (18% of the recipe). Name matching is case-insensitive and must match the product name as it appears in the ledger below. Leave a product out and it just won't get a batch count.
